Lempel–Ziv music synthesis

Copying Versus Randomization in Lempel-Ziv Music Synthesis

Nadav Mishan · Ram Zamir

ABSTRACT

We use Lempel–Ziv universal compression to generate musical notes and control the algorithm’s tendency to over-copy or under-copy training data by changing the average sequence length stored in its dictionary.

Presented at ICSEE · IEEE Xplore publication forthcoming.
